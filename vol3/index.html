FAQ

Vol. 3 questions

What is included in this volume?

This 30-page ebook contains 20 melodic lines in standard notation and guitar tablature, with detailed fingering and picking instructions. It also includes chord voicing suggestions. Audio recordings at 50, 100, and 150 BPM and slow video demonstrations accompany the examples.

Who is Modern Linear Vocabulary for?

The series is for musicians who want to move beyond familiar scale patterns and develop a more personal approach to improvisation. The notation and tablature are designed for guitar, while the melodic concepts can also inspire players of other instruments. Work at your own pace and begin with short fragments before tackling longer lines.

How should I practice the lines?

Start with short fragments and practice slowly. Focus on the fingering and picking instructions for both hands until each movement feels comfortable. Then practice with the included audio, beginning at 50 BPM and increasing the tempo gradually. Listen closely to the phrasing as well as the notes.

Can I use these ideas in different musical styles?

Yes. Adapt the melodic ideas to the style you play by changing their rhythm, phrasing, and articulation. You do not need to play every line at a fast tempo: try short melodic fragments, leave space between phrases, and combine single-note lines with chords.

Do I need to learn every example?

No. Choose the lines that appeal to you, combine fragments from different examples, and use them to create your own phrases. The goal is to develop musical freedom and a vocabulary that reflects your own voice.

Do I need to follow the suggested fingering?

You can adapt the fingerings, but the suggested choices help develop efficient movement, clear articulation, and fluid phrasing across the fretboard. They also give you musical material for practicing techniques such as hybrid picking. Start with the written fingerings, then explore alternatives that suit your hands and musical ideas.
